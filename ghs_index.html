Global Higgsy Seminar

The Global Higgsy Seminar is an informal geometry seminar with a loose focus on Higgs bundles, held online roughly every second Thursday during the semester. The Zoom link can be obtained by reaching out to one of the organizers, who are Raphael Belliard, Eric Boulter, and Evan Sundbo.

Upcoming Talks:

  • May 14 at 10:00 AM CST - Wennan Zhang
    Title: Tangency Operations in Free Loop spaces
    Abstract: In this talk, I will discuss a string-topology construction on the free loop space LM of a smooth manifold M, using a chosen nowhere-vanishing vector field v. The basic idea is to study loops whose tangent direction agrees with the direction of M. I will first explain the ordinary tangency condition, then introduce the positive tangency refinement. This construction is partly motivated by open holomorphic curve theory, where boundary loops in a Lagrangian interact with chosen nowhere-vanishing vector fields in four-chain and linking constructions. I will describe the resulting chain-level operations and explain why the positive tangency operator becomes a chain map when M is odd-dimensional.
