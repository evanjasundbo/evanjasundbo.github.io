Global Higgsy Seminar

The Global Higgsy Seminar is an informal geometry seminar with a loose focus on Higgs bundles, held online roughly every second Thursday during the semester. The Zoom link can be obtained by reaching out to one of the organizers, who are Raphael Belliard, Eric Boulter, and Evan Sundbo.

Upcoming Talks:

  • May 28th at 10:00 AM CST - David Sun
    Title: p-adic integration and BPS-invariants of character stacks
    Abstract: In this talk, I will give a brief overview of p-adic integration, motivated by a series of results of Groechenig, Wyss, and Ziegler. In particular, I will introduce their BPS-invariants for “nice” moduli stacks, which is connected to Donaldson–Thomas theory. I will then describe a method for evaluating BPS-invariants of G-character stacks for reductive groups G. Finally, I will present some small computational results, including the BPS-invariant of the GL2​-character stack matches the E-polynomial of the twisted GL2​-character variety computed by Hausel and Rodríguez-Villegas, as well as a possible mirror symmetry statement relating the SL2​-character stack and the twisted PGL2​-character stack.
